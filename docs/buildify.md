### Requirements

KISAN SETHU - Smart Farmer Procurement Slot Booking System for Smart India Hackathon 2026

**Core Goal**: Help farmers avoid long queues by booking agricultural procurement slots digitally.

**Key Features**:
- Farmer registration and phone-based login
- Find nearby procurement centers with real-time slot availability
- Check current crop procurement prices and accepted crops
- Enter crop quantity and pesticide details
- Book time slots with 3-step wizard
- Track booking status through completion
- Generate digital receipts after sale

**User Base**: Small and marginal farmers with limited technical knowledge

### Designs

**Color Palette**:
- Primary: Green (#22C55E) - agricultural, trust, growth
- Accent: Orange (#F59E0B) - action, attention
- Background: White (#FFFFFF) - clean, readable
- Text: Dark gray (#1F2937) - high contrast

**Typography**:
- Large buttons and clear labels for farmer accessibility
- Minimal text, maximum clarity
- Agriculture-themed visual language

**Mobile-first**: All screens optimized for 375px width
